### Example: Comparative Manifestos (CMP)
#### Methodology: hand-coding of manifestos
- Divide manifesto into ‘quasi-sentences’
- Assign quasi-sentences to one category
- Compute percentages of in relation to whole text
#### Categories: 56 items grouped in 7 policy domains
- E.g., external relations, economy, fabric of society, ... ); selection of 26 categories constitute left-right scale.
- Coverage: Europe, Latin America, Asia; 60 countries, since 1950s, ca. 3000 party manifestos; see CMP.
### Example: Chapel Hill Expert Surveys (CHES)
#### Methodology: expert classification of parties
- Questionnaire to country experts.
- Locate parties on a scale, e.g. 1 to 10.
- Compute party averages across country experts
#### Dimensions: overall ideological position (left-right)
- And position on economic issues (econ left-right); position on democratic freedoms and rights (green, alternative, libertarian (GAL)-traditional, authoritarian, nationalist (TAN)); ...
- Coverage: Europe, Latin America, Canada; 45 countries, several surveys since 1999, >250 parties (last survey); see CHES.
