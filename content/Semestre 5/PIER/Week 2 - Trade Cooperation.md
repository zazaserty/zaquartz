### The rise of liberalism
![[Pasted image 20260924104027.png]]
Critique of the gold standard, having more gold doesn't equate to being wealthier. Gold is only useful if you use it, but just having sitting around means you are under consuming. At best, it's a metric of how much you can consume.

This is a positive sum world, not a zero sum one like with mercantilism.
