## Globalisation
### Anti-globalisation movement
- 1980ff: growing critique.
- 1999: spotlight on ‘Battle of Seattle’.
- 2000-2007: regular protests at WTO, IMF, World Bank, G8 meetings.
- Post-2008: anti-Wall-Street / anti-austerity protests; populist party success.
### Two (interrelated) critiques
- Inequality: wealth gap (within / across countries); economic dependency; power asymmetry; environmental justice; ‘race to the bottom’.
- Democracy / Sovereignty: loss of democratic accountability; IO influence / transparency; corporate influence; policy constraints (see ‘race to the bottom’).
